Subscription help

To manage or cancel Xayra Premium, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Canceling prevents future renewals; your Premium access normally continues until the end of the current entitlement period.

Common questions

How do I export my notes?

In Xayra, open Settings → Export my notes. It saves all your notes and to-dos as a plain-text file in a folder you choose. The file isn’t encrypted, so anyone who can open it can read your notes. Keep it somewhere safe, and delete it when you no longer need it.

How do I delete my notes?

Tap the bin icon on a note in Archive or Records to delete it. To delete every note on the phone, use Settings → Delete All Notes. See Data Deletion for removing backups and subscription records.

What if I lose my Extra Privacy recovery code?

Keep your recovery code somewhere safe. It isn’t uploaded to Google Drive, and Xayra support can’t recover it or open backups locked with it. A phone that can already open those backups can still open them, and you can start new Standard backups of the notes on your phone. Backups that exist only under the lost code may be impossible to recover.

Privacy and data requests

You can also contact support@xayra.org about privacy or subscription-record deletion requests. Notes stored solely on your phone aren't accessible to Xayra support.

Please don't email passwords, recovery codes, payment-card details or private note contents.
