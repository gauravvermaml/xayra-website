Privacy Policy

Xayra — Your Pocket Companion · Last updated: [publication date]

At a glance

Xayra processes voice notes, transcripts, searches and AI answers on your Android phone. Xayra does not send that personal content to a remote AI service or use advertising or usage analytics. Optional Google Drive, Google Calendar and Premium subscription features involve the service providers described below.

1. Notes and on-device processing

Voice recognition, search and answers operate on your phone. Your notes, questions, transcripts and to-dos are not sent to a developer-operated server or a remote AI provider. Xayra downloads its AI models when needed from Cloudflare and Hugging Face. Like other downloads, those services may receive technical request information such as your IP address and device information.

2. Storage and security

Newer versions of Xayra store notes and to-dos in an encrypted database in the app's private storage. Older Xayra versions used different storage protections; existing files or backups from those versions may remain until removed. An export you save as a text file is not encrypted by Xayra.

3. Optional Google services

If you connect Google Drive, Xayra uses a private application-data folder to store backups in your own Google account. Backup and restore actions occur when you request them. If you connect Google Calendar and send a to-do, Xayra sends the information needed to create or manage that calendar event. Your Google Drive account may differ from the Google Play account used for purchases.

4. Backup protection and recovery

Standard Protection: Backups are encrypted, but the information required to restore them is also stored in your Google account's Xayra app data. Someone with sufficient access to that account data may be able to read them.

Extra Privacy: You can optionally protect backups with a recovery code that is not uploaded to Google Drive. If you lose your recovery code and have no device able to unlock the backup, protected backups may be unrecoverable, including by Xayra support.

You can turn off Extra Privacy for future backups. Existing protected backups remain protected. If you have lost your code but can still access notes on your phone, Xayra may let you start a new Standard backup of those local notes. It cannot automatically unlock notes that exist only in an inaccessible protected backup.

5. Xayra Premium subscriptions

Google Play handles subscription billing and payment information. Xayra uses RevenueCat to check subscriptions and enable paid features. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app customer identifier and subscription-related information, such as plan, price, purchase and renewal status, together with limited technical data needed to provide the service. Xayra does not send RevenueCat the content of your notes, recordings, questions, to-dos or backups.

Subscription activity may be used to view overall purchase and subscription counts. Xayra does not use advertising or usage analytics and disables RevenueCat's automatic device-identifier collection and diagnostics in its SDK configuration.

When Premium ends, your existing notes and backups remain available. You may manage or cancel your subscription through Google Play.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect optional Google connections, delete local notes, export your notes, and remove Xayra's hidden backup data through Google Drive's app-data controls. Deleting local data does not automatically delete copies you previously backed up or exported. To ask about RevenueCat subscription-record deletion, email support@xayra.org. We may require information sufficient to identify the purchase without requesting your payment card details.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Xayra may request microphone, notifications, biometric authentication, internet access, Google Drive app-data access, Google Calendar permissions and Google Play Billing. Xayra does not request the advertising-ID permission.

8. Children

Xayra is not directed at children.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when our practices change. For questions about privacy, contact support@xayra.org.
