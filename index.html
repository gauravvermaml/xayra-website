Your pocket companion

Your thoughts, ready when you need them.

Record a thought, save a detail, and find it later. Xayra helps you remember what matters, with AI-powered features that work on your Android phone.

Get in touch

Xayra is for Android and is currently in testing on Google Play. It isn’t publicly released yet.

Made for everyday memories

Capture naturally

Speak or type a note when inspiration strikes. When your hands are busy, turn on Handsfree and say “Xayra” followed by your thought. It listens only while Handsfree is on and Xayra is open on your screen, ignores speech without “Xayra”, and turns off when you leave the app.

Ask your notes

Ask a question and Xayra answers from your notes, on your phone. It checks each answer against your notes before showing it and lists the notes it checked, so you can open the originals. If it can’t verify an answer, it says so and shows those notes instead.

Search your words

Type a word to find it in Archive, the full-screen Record view or To-Do. Join words with + to find entries that contain all of them, in any order: soccer+eli finds notes that mention both.

Your data, your choice

Notes are stored in an encrypted database on your phone. Google Drive backup is optional and encrypted, and Extra Privacy can also lock backups with a recovery code that isn’t uploaded. Calendar connections are optional too.

Privacy is part of the design.

Your notes and questions aren't sent to a remote AI service. Some features, including subscriptions and optional Google connections, rely on external services. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
